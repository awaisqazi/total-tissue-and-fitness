What's the session frequency?

The frequency of Total Tissue sessions should be scheduled based on age and personal assessment. For younger individuals (under 30), a session every six months can be sufficient. For individuals between 30 and 50, it's advisable to schedule a session every three months. For those above 50, a more frequent schedule, such as every two months, can be beneficial. However, these are general guidelines and personal assessment by a professional can lead to a more individualized schedule, taking into account personal health conditions and lifestyle factors.
‍

Can you help with Sciatica, Plantar Fasciitis, Pelvic floor, Hyper-mobility, etc?  

Total Tissue sessions can aid conditions like Sciatica, Plantar Fasciitis, Pelvic Floor disorders, and Hyper-mobility by releasing tension and improving blood flow. They can reduce nerve pressure, relax muscles, and strengthen tissues, easing pain and enhancing mobility. However, they should complement a comprehensive treatment plan supervised by a healthcare professional.
